[h1]优妮、可可萝（公主）、镜华领导的兰德索尔：先睹为快[/h1]

优妮、可可萝、镜华位列《公主连结 Re:Dive》最受欢迎的角色之中。本领袖包包含了三位体型娇小的领袖，在儿童节到来之际为《文明6》添加一抹童趣。

[b]兰德索尔[/b]的文明特性[b]“公主骑士”[/b]是一种特别的能力，对于每位领导兰德索尔的领袖而言，它都提供截然不同的加成。他们的特色建筑是[b]公会之家[/b]，可为城市提供额外的生产力，并为每个领袖提供独特的加成。兰德索尔特色启蒙时代单位[b]王宫骑士团炮[/b]相比其替代的射石炮拥有更强的战斗力，且不需要战略资源。

[b]优妮[/b]的特色能力是[b]“博学者的教诲”[/b]。建设图书馆能够提供额外的大作家点数和文学著作槽位，文学著作则提供额外的科技值和文化值。她同时解锁[b]“优妮旋翼机”[/b]单位，比其所替代的无人机要更早解锁且更为廉价。

对于优妮，公主骑士特性提供[b]“认知发展哲学”[/b]，令学院、剧院广场和战略资源同时提供科技值和文化值，且单位在生产或升级完成后能获得一次免费的晋升。

[b]公主可可萝[/b]的独特能力是[b]“纯白天启”[/b]。若与山脉、森林或自然奇观相邻，单元格产出额外信仰。圣地可提升城市单元格魅力。公主可可萝还拥有“爱梅斯巫女”单位，这一平民单位在君权神授市政即可解锁，可以建设国家公园或者植树造林。

“公主骑士”特性为她提供的是[b]“极光圣域”[/b]，让他们善于利用自然的力量。对他们而言，未改良的自然风光是很好的食物和生产力来源。如果他们在城市中建设区域建筑或国家公园，还可以从这些单元格获得更为可观的收益。在魅力较高的单元格，单位可以获得额外回复。（若您没有越南DLC，她的能力会有所改变。详情请查看游戏内描述。）

[b]镜华[/b]的独特能力是[b]“小小优等生”[/b]。拥有学院区域的城市获得额外科技值和生产力。若城市拥有较多海岸单元格，则这些产出还会进一步强化。

“公主骑士”特性为她提供的是[b]“宇宙蓝闪光”[/b]，令水域为他们提供大量战术和战略优势。学院可以从河流及海岸获得额外相邻加成。沿河或沿海的陆地远程单位可获得额外射程，单元格则有额外的生产力。一旦他们在太空竞赛项目中获得进展，还可以免费获得一项对胜利至关重要的科技。

您能够成为小小的伟大领袖吗？您将会如何带领兰德索尔建立经得起时间考验的文明？

[h1]我们的风格：[/h1]

[b]有玩法，不超模。[/b]每位领袖的能力组都经过精心设计与测试，使它们能够支持一种独特的玩法。能力强度与官方的强力领袖相当而不会击碎原作平衡。

[b]忠于原作。[/b]我们的作品在制作过程中精细考证了领袖和他们的故事，拥有完整的人名、地名、城市名称和外交用语，以尽最大可能表现原作的风土人情和领袖性格特征。

[b]广泛兼容。[/b]所有已制作的美术素材均参照《文明6》游戏的规格进行处理，确保其在各种分辨率和设置下都能正确显示且整洁清爽。

[b]背景音乐和领袖语音。[/b]是谁找到了在Wwise Soundbank中设置这些音频的正确播放方式？是的，就是我！

[b]负责任的AI使用。[/b]我们谨慎地使用生成式AI创作的内容，确保不含“数字泔水”。

[h1]已知问题：[/h1]

文学著作提供的额外科技值可能与其他模组冲突，致使其效果无法正常工作。

音乐播放有时会存在问题，具体表现与官方的越南和葡萄牙文明相同。

文明百科仅支持简体中文。

爱梅斯巫女的建造次数或创建国家公园次数的任意一个耗尽均会导致该单位被解散。

本模组替换了加载界面和外交界面以隐藏不需要的“兰德索尔公主骑士”字样。若您安装有修改以上界面的模组，您将会看到本条特性，但没有更多效果。

[h1]Yuni, Kokkoro (Princess) and Kyoka Lead Landosol: First Look[/h1]

Yuni, Kokkoro and Kyoka are some of the most popular characters in the mobile game Princess Connect Re:Dive. This mod contains three little leaders who lead Landosol, and brings some cuteness to Civilization VI to celebrate China's Children's Day (June 1).

The unique ability of Landosol civilization [b]Princess Knight[/b] is a special ability that provides entirely different abilities to each Landosol leader. Their unique building is [b]Guildhouse[/b] which provides Production and unique bonuses for each Landosol leader. Landosol unique Renaissance Era unit [b]Royal Palace Cannon[/b] has stronger Combat Strength than Bombard it replaces and does not need any Strategic resource.

[b]Yuni[/b]'s unique ability is [b]Knowledge of Erudites[/b]. Constructing Library provides extra Great Writer Points and Writing great work slot, and Writing great works provide additional Science and Culture. She also unlocks [b]Yunicopter[/b], which is available earlier and cheaper than the Drone unit it replaces. 

For Yuni, Princess Knight grants [b]Philosophy of Cognition and Development[/b], which allows Campuses, Theater Squares and Strategic resources provide Science and Culture at the same time, and let units receive a free Promotion when they are produced or upgraded.

The [b]Princess Persona of Kokkoro[/b] has a unique ability named [b]Pure White Revelation[/b]. Tiles provide additional Faith when adjacent to Mountains, Woods, or Natural Wonders. Holy Sites increase Appeal of cities they are in. Princess Kokkoro also have the Miko of Ameth unique unit. This civilian unit is unlocked with Divine Right, and can create a National Park or plant Woods. 

Princess Knight grants her [b]Aurora Sanctuary[/b], allowing them to harness natural power. For them, unimproved natural scenery is a good source of Food and Production. They can gain even more from these tiles if they construct districts, buildings or national parks. Units also heal faster in tiles with high Appeal. (Her ability changes if you don't have Vietnam DLC. For details check in-game description.)

The unique ability of Kyoka is [b]Little Model Student[/b]. Cities with a Campus district will have increased output of Science and Production. These yields are further enhanced if the city owns many Coast tiles.

Princess Knight grants her [b]Cosmo Blue Flash[/b], allowing water to provide them with tactical and strategic advantages. Campuses are granted an extra adjacency with Rivers and from Coasts. When adjacent to a River or Coast, Ranged units receive Range bonus, and tiles can have additional Production. When they make progress in Space Race projects, they can unlock a free technology vital for winning.

Can you become a little great leader? How will you lead Landosol to build a civilization that stands the test of time?

[h1]Our Way of Modding:[/h1]

[b]Yes for Uniqueness, No for Overpowering[/b]: All leaders are designed and tested so that each of their ability sets promotes a unique play-style. They are set to be as powerful as strong official leaders and won't break game balancing.

[b]Lore-Loyalty[/b]: Our mods are made with a fine research of the leaders and their stories, and provides full lists of named places, citizen names, city names and diplomacy statements to better represent lore and leader characteristics.

[b]Optimized for Compatibility[/b]: All art assets included in this mod are processed to fit the need of Civilization VI, so that they can display correctly and neatly on different screens / settings.

[b]Background Music and Leader Speeches[/b]: Who found the correct way to properly set these audio in Wwise Soundbanks? Yes, that was me!

[b]Responsible AI Usage[/b]: We use generative-AI-created contents with caution to ensure the mod is slop-free.

[h1]Known issues:[/h1]

Extra Science from Writing great works will conflict with other mods that also provide the same bonus, thus causing Science output of Writing great works to be calculated erroneously.

Music playback can be sometimes glitchy, with the same issues of some official civilizations like Vietnam and Portugal.

Some Civilopedia articles are in Simplified Chinese only.

The Miko of Ameth unit will be disbanded once either Build Charges or National Park Charge is depleted.

This mod replaces loading screen and diplomacy screen to hide unneeded Princess Knight entries (as it becomes a unique ability of each leader). If you enabled mods that replace them, you will see a redundant unique ability without extra effects.
